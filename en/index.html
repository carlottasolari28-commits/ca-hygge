why “hygge”?

A seaside house that feels like home

hygge · Danish word
the feeling of warmth and wellbeing found in simple things: a blanket, soft light, time spent together.

That's what we wanted Ca' Hygge to be: a place to put your bags down and feel at ease straight away. Three bedrooms, a bright living room, a kitchen to really cook in, and the sea close by.

  • Tips and directions also left in writing
  • Plants, natural light and warm wooden floors
  • Board games for evenings in
  • Your dog is welcome too

The rooms

Living room

The heart of the house: bright, with warm wooden floors, the sofa and the white staircase leading up to the attic room. In the evening: Netflix and Prime Video on the TV, a board game, or planning tomorrow's trip.

  • Sofa bed

Kitchen and dining

A white, fully equipped kitchen with coffee machine, kettle and microwave, and a round table for a slow breakfast before heading to the beach.

Double bedroom

Spacious and quiet, with two windows that fill it with light. Besides the double bed there is a comfortable sofa bed.

  • Double bed
  • Sofa bed

Attic room

The hideaway under the wooden roof, with a skylight: the favourite room for those who like a lie-in.

  • French double bed

Small bedroom

Small and cosy, with black-and-white prints on the walls: perfect for a child or a solo traveller.

  • Single bed

Bathroom

With shower and bidet. Towels, bed linen and a courtesy set are waiting for you, and there's a washing machine in the house.
